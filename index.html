ARRIVAL

You land on the rocky shore and climb toward the old observatory. Nobody has lived here for fifty years. Salt has eaten the railings, a buoy bell tolls somewhere in the dark, and the lighthouse beam sweeps over the rocks every nine seconds. Nine, you notice. Always nine.

This is the place where the research team vanished overnight in 1974. Somewhere inside these ruins, a receiver is waiting — and the signal has started again.
